Very excited to be part of this panel discussion about diverse career choices (art vs. technology) and its impact on lives. Do join us. We would love to see you.

ANVI-IAWW follows its hugely successful event last month where we focused on Creative Pursuits with Part 2 of this series. Join us for a panel discussion featuring Srivani Jade, Joyce Paul and Meera Krishna and moderated by Mandira Virmani. This will be followed by a breathtaking performance by these super talented artists.

The evening begins with networking at 6 and ends with a Q&A after the performance. Mark your calendars!
