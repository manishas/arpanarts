      At Arpan we provide a multitude of learning opportunities to suit your

  - _template: cards
    columns: '1'
    items:
      - title: Quarterly Group Lessons
        href: /classes/quarterly-classes
        body: >-
          Most registered students are auto-enrolled for quarterly classes. They
          become part of a group that starts at the same level and proceeds to
          learn all aspects of the art form as a team; supporting and nurturing
          each other.
      - title: Anytime Private or Semi-private Lessons
        href: /classes/private-classes
        body: |-
          We also offer private classes for those occasions that require personal 1:1 attention. Some typical scenarios include:

          * Preparations for shows or arangetrams.
          * Desire to work on polishing technique, abhinaya, theory or any other aspect.
          * Need more support to assimilate what was taught in class.
          * Willingness to learn an aspect that is not in their curriculum yet.
          * Desire to learn a different repertoire piece or work on choreographing an item as per personal interest.
      - title: Visiting Artists & Living Legends Series
        body: >-
          We are always interested and excited to bring visiting artists to
          enhance and enrich Arpan student experiences. Some names that have
          graced the Arpan studio are Prof. C.V. Chandrasekhar, Astad Deboo, Dr.
          Kalamandalam Radhika, Shri V.P. Dhananjayan, Shaly Vijayan, Sunil
          Kothari, Uma Nagar, Mohanan and Seshadri Iyengar amongst others.
      - title: Cross-Genre Work
        body: >-
          Cross-genre work is encouraged and promoted at Arpan. Students have had
          the opportunity to interact with dancers from different genres such as
          Kathak, Mohiniyattam, Odissi, Contemporary (both Indian and American),
          African American folk and Ballet.
      - title: Mommy and Me
        body: >-
          Mommy and me classes are yet another popular option where mothers can
          learn with their daughters or use the private lessons to bond with
          their child during the learning process by participating in a focussed
          manner. This class is available not just to tiny tots but also to
          teenagers and their moms as they navigate through a difficult period in
          their lives.
