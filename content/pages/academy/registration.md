    body: |-
      Registrations are open throughout the year for students with some training in Bharatanatyam or Mohiniyattam.

      For students new to either art form, admissions are typically open in August for Fall start in September. You need to have attended a Meet & Greet and completed the registration process in August to be able to attend the September session.

      The first step towards becoming an ARPANITE officially is to complete a one-time registration with a non-refundable $50 registration fee that is valid until and unless you voluntarily cancel your membership at Arpan.

      **Follow these steps to register**

      1. Click the Registration PDF link below.
      2. Print the document.
      3. Fill, sign and date.
      4. Attach a non-refundable check towards the registration fee. (Online payment information can be made available during your Meet & Greet or Audition date.)
      5. Deposit personally at Arpan Studios or mail to 24311 NE 80th St, Redmond, WA 98053.

      **Bring a friend and get a discount.** If your friend registers, you will receive a $25 discount on a private class session with any instructor at Arpan!
