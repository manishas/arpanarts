blocks:
  - _template: steps
    heading: Admission Process
    items:
      - title: Audition
        body: >-
          In the audition process, you meet a member of the faculty to determine
          if Arpan meets your dance goals and if you as a student are a good
          cultural fit at Arpan. The audition can be in the form of a 1-on-1
          session or conducted during an Open House / Meet & Greet session. Click
          [here](/academy/audition) for more information on how to audition at
          Arpan.
      - title: Level Set
        body: >-
          Note what level you will be admitted at. Once you have auditioned, Dr.
          Joyce will recommend a level for you to start off based on your skill
          levels and the Arpan curriculum. You may have gaps in your training
          that put you between two levels. We will come up with a learning path
          to help you fill those gaps. It is up to you to make that commitment
          and get on par with your classmates as soon as possible. Click
          [here](/classes/syllabus#nrittabeginner) for more information on
          levels.
      - title: Register
        body: >-
          Fill, sign and date the registration forms and waiver. You are now
          officially a student and will have access to internal class documents,
          rules and guidelines. Complete information on the registration process
          can be found [here](/academy/registration).
      - title: Payment
        body: >-
          You can pay in cash, check or online. Once you are registered as a
          student, quarter details will be available to you. Generally speaking a
          Level 1 group class is about $250 for a quarter and might vary a bit
          depending on the number of weeks in a quarter.
