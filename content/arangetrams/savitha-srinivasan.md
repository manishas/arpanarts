ARPAN is super proud to present Savitha Srinivasan in her solo debut recital in Bharatanatyam.

### Special piece

As always Dr. Joyce Paul encourages her students to have a special piece in their Arangetram that represents a concept close to their hearts.

Savitha has always been impressed with Van Gogh and tonight she will regale the audience with a piece on the torture he faced internally and externally. Being a budding poet herself, Savitha was encouraged by her guru to write poetry about her feelings for Van Gogh.

All are welcome. Entry is free.
