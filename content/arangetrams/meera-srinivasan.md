ARPAN is proud to present its first young adult arangetram by Meera Srinivasan.

### Special piece

As always Dr. Joyce Paul encourages her students to have a special piece in their Arangetram that represents a concept close to their hearts.

Meera chose to pick "The Giving Tree" by Shel Silverstein and choreographed most of the piece all on her own at the tender age of 13!
