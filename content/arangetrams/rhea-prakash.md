ARPAN is super proud and Rhea is super excited to present her solo debut representing close to 10 years of training with her guru to the community in Seattle.

### Repertoire

Rhea's repertoire includes new works such as

* A new varnam about the unsung strong women in the bible and their trials, tribulations and faith.
* A new javali "Yenthadi Kulukke"

### Special piece

As always Dr. Joyce Paul encourages her students to have a special piece in their Arangetram that represents a concept close to their hearts. Rhea has picked a song that her Guru used to sing as a youngster in Delhi - "Vannu Keralathil". Rhea will use her choreography skills learnt at Arpan to create this work.

All are welcome. Entry is free.
