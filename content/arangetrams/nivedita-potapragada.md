ARPAN is super proud to present Nivedita Potapragada in her solo debut recital in Bharatanatyam.

### Special piece

As always Dr. Joyce Paul encourages her students to have a special piece in their Arangetram that represents a concept close to their hearts.

Nivedita chose to pick a strong woman. Being from Karnataka, her guru recommended picking a historic or current character and weaving a narrative of feminine strength around this character. The family picked Queen Shanthala of the Hoysala dynasty and her own Mother and Aunt wrote the poetry for her main piece - the Varnam.

All are welcome. Entry is free.
