ARPAN is super proud and Akshata is super excited to present her solo debut representing close to 10 years of training with her guru to the community here in Seattle.

### Repertoire

Akshata's repertoire includes new works such as

* A new crisp Sankeerna Alarippu created by her guru that experiments with new movements while keeping the skeletal framework of the traditional alarippu intact.
* Netr varen padam with a fresh look at the poetry and how it can be interpreted for the dancer in question.
* A new Javali "adi neepai marulu" that addresses concepts and ideas pertinent even today.
* A brand new varnam about the Saint Poetess Andaal with lyrics written by Akshata's own father, Aravind Seshadri!!! What a blast we had creating this piece of work.

### Special piece

As always Dr. Joyce Paul encourages her students to have a special piece in their Arangetram that represents a concept close to their hearts. Akshata has decided to do a piece about how human beings hold on to their masks as they live through life and try to be happy. She will be writing the lyrics and composing music to her ideas.

All are welcome. Entry is free.
