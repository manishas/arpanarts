ARPAN is super proud and Devanshi is super excited to present her solo debut representing years of intense training with her guru to the community here in Seattle. We have 4 new guest artists participating in the show (outside of the regular orchestra coming in from Chennai). This is a first and an exciting one with 5 new choreographic works!!

### Special piece

As always Dr. Joyce Paul encourages her students to have a special piece in their Arangetram that represents a concept close to their hearts. Devanshi has decided to present a piece on her favourite person, Mother Teresa of Calcutta.

Other new choreographic works include

1. A vandanaa to Maa Shaarada in Raag Bhairavi
2. Vaishnav jan - Gandhiji's favourite bhajan in Bharatanatyam format
3. A beautiful sensual padam by Dr. Balamurali Krishna - "Marulu Minchera"
4. A heartbreaking ashtapadi - "Hari Hari Hatadharataya" set to exquisite music by Srivani Jade

All are welcome. Entry is free.
